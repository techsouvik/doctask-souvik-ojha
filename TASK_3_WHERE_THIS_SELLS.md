* **Document Pile:** Master Construction Agreements, FIDIC/EPC Contracts, Bill of Quantities (BOQ), Change Orders, Architect Progress Reports.
* **The Problem:** Commercial construction projects (like tech parks or metro lines) run on hundreds of contract amendments and progress certifications. Overbilling against incomplete milestones and unapproved contractor variations cause millions in disputes.
* **The SuperDocs Solution:** SuperDocs ingests contractor invoices and status reports, compares billed percentages against architect certifications, proposes section-level credit note corrigendums, and exports clean, audited redline DOCX files for legal approval.
* **Named Target Buyers:**
  * **Larsen & Toubro (L&T Construction)** — Head of Project Controls / Commercial Director
  * **DLF Limited** — VP of Contracts & Procurement
  * **Godrej Properties** — Chief Legal Officer / General Counsel
  * **Turner Construction (USA)** — VP of Risk & Claims
* **Economic ROI:** Prevents 2–5% unearned milestone overbilling on $50M+ projects; eliminates 30+ legal billable hours per major dispute.
